- "Registrations" is the count on 2026-11-01. Registrations are always higher than final submissions, and the count keeps growing until the deadline, so treat the ratio as an upper bound on odds, not a promise.

- Rows 9 and 10 are crypto-linked (CRYPTO). Not recommended unless you want that track.
